NASA SPACE APPS 2026

APOLLO 11

Plan it. Fly it. Land it.
A real Moon mission, from launch day to "The Eagle has landed", in about five minutes.

01 · THE PROBLEM

Space history is read, not lived

  • Students learn Apollo as dates and facts
  • They never feel the decisions under pressure
  • They never see why one choice saves the mission and another ends it
02 · PLAN

You are the planner

  • Launch day, real Sun and Moon (NASA JPL)
  • Any landing site on the Moon
  • Eagle's fuel vs. Saturn V margin
  • Free-return or hybrid route
  • Go / No-Go poll
03 · REAL DATA

Real weather, real ground

  • NASA POWER: 45 years of Kennedy weather
  • Rain clouds can trigger lightning, as on Apollo 12
  • NASA LOLA heights and LROC colour at your site
  • Real 1969 mission radio
04 · PROBLEMS

Real failures, your call

  • Apollo 11 · 1202 alarm
  • Apollo 12 · lightning, dust
  • Apollo 13 · engine out
  • Apollo 14 · stuck latches, radar, fake abort
  • Wrong choice: you see what happens, learn why, rewind
05 · LAND

You fly the last 150 m

  • Boulders, slopes, dust
  • Fuel counting down to BINGO
  • No score: a summary of what you did
06 · IMPACT

Every run is different

  • Date, site, weather and choices change the mission
  • Learning by deciding, not memorising
  • Built in Unity, runs on a laptop
